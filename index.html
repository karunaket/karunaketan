Workflow

My Process

🔍

Research

Breaking down games to see what makes their core loops, combat feel, and pacing click.

📝

Brief

Setting clear Design Pillars early so every mechanic supports the core player fantasy.

💡

Ideation

Sketching maps and math on paper first to fail fast before touching the engine.

📖

Docs

Writing visual GDDs and state diagrams that programmers can actually build from.

⚙️

Prototype

Building playable systems in Unreal Engine 5 Blueprints to test feel right away.

🎮

Testing

Watching players break things in playtests and tuning curves based on real behavior.

🚀

Release

Packaging builds, fixing performance bottlenecks, and making the first 5 minutes smooth.

📊

Analysis

Reviewing playtest feedback and writing post-mortems on what worked and what broke.
