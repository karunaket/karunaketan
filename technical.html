AOA: Space Scrap Smugglers

Role: Contract Game Designer • Studio: FatCatPlay (Team: 12+) • Duration: 3 Months • Engine: Unreal Engine 5
The Hook • Technical Outcome

Engineered physics-based tool synergies that forced verbal and non-verbal player coordination, cutting player "lonewolfing" by 50% in playtests.

The Technical Problem

Designing co-op synergy where players must physically depend on each other without overloading them with complex controls, input lag, or intrusive text tutorials.

Systemic & Architectural Execution
  • "Mass Dependency" Blueprint Rule: Architected physics interactions in UE5 where heavy scrap items dynamically calculate combined vector pull forces—requiring 2+ players with Gravity Beams to stabilize and transport.
  • Decoupled Component Synergies: Utilized event dispatchers and modular actor components rather than hard-coded casting, allowing rapid iteration on tool synergies.
  • Programmer-Facing Technical GDDs: Authored unambiguous design specifications with state transition diagrams used directly by engineering sprints.

Shattered Charms: Through The Veil

Role: Systems & Economy Designer • Scope: Solo Project • Duration: 3 Months • Engine: Unreal Engine 5
The Hook • Technical Outcome

Balanced build diversity and eliminated dead-end stats across multi-tier progression curves through rigorous spreadsheet modeling before prototyping in engine.

The Technical Problem

Preventing exponential mathematical power creep, currency inflation, and dominant min-max strategies in an Action RPG gear and combat loop.

Systemic & Architectural Execution
  • Excel Mathematical Modeling: Formulated mathematical progression curves, 3-tier purification exchange ratios, currency sinks, and drop-rate probability matrices.
  • Modular "Charm Matrix" Logic: Built an un-gated modular progression system replacing rigid skill trees, allowing dynamic multi-slot stat synergy combinations.
  • UE5 Combat Loop Prototype: Prototyped the combat synergy loop and boss encounter states in Unreal Engine 5 to validate numerical balance against real-time game feel.

Eclipse of Fear

Role: AI & Systems Designer • Team: 2 Members • Duration: 1 Month • Engine: Unreal Engine 5
The Hook • Technical Outcome

Adaptive psychological horror tension that dynamically responds to player survival markers without relying on scripted, predictable jump scares.

The Technical Problem

Scripted horror encounters rapidly lose player tension upon replay once players identify static trigger volumes and predictable enemy timing.

Systemic & Architectural Execution
  • Dynamic "Intensity Director" AI: Architected reactive AI in UE5 Behavior Trees and Blackboard data keys that scales enemy aggression, stalk distance, and pacing based on player survival metrics.
  • Diegetic Event System: Engineered the "Lives as Papers" mechanic where player damage fires an event to dynamically dissolve a physical narrative quest document in real time.
  • Data-Driven Wave Balancing: Balanced 4 distinct enemy waves using Unreal Data Sheets to fine-tune sightlines, movement speed, and detection cones.
