AOA: Space Scrap Smugglers

A physics-driven co-op space mining and smuggling game built with a 12+ person studio team at FatCatPlay. I designed cooperative physics rules, Smelter extraction pacing, textless onboarding, and visual GDDs for engineering.

Genre: Co-Op Physics / Space Extraction Role: Contract Game Designer Team: 12+ (FatCatPlay) Duration: 3 Months Engine: Unreal Engine 5

★ Key Design & Technical Highlights

  • Designed the Gravity Beam Mass Dependency rule so heavy salvage physically requires two players to coordinate instead of solo-carrying.
  • Wrote modular visual GDDs used daily by programmers, artists, and QA across a 12+ person studio team.
  • Built a textless onboarding flow inside a Hub-and-Spoke station using diegetic crosshair and color cues instead of tutorial popups.
  • Specified ship inertia physics, crate-carrying weight rules, and the Smelter Urgency Loop to keep extraction runs tense.

03. Station Flow & Textless Onboarding

We built the playable station as a Hub-and-Spoke layout anchored around the central smuggling ship. Teams venture down outward wings for higher-risk loot and always funnel back toward the central extraction bay, keeping everyone oriented without minimaps.

05. Technical Design: Physics Rules, Smelter Loop & Diegetic UI

To turn our GDD sheets into clear engineering specs for programmers, I broke down every player interaction into clear physics states, crosshair feedback rules, and Smelter processing timers:

Technical Spec // Core Gameplay Loop & Interaction Rules
  • Diegetic Crosshair & Object Outlines: Instead of floating text prompts, hovering over salvage shifts the center reticle and highlights the crate in green (solo lift) or amber (requires a second Gravity Beam).
  • Zero-G Thruster Inertia: Tuned drift decay and braking impulses so players feel real weight when hauling salvage through narrow station corridors.
  • Smelter Processing Urgency: Dropping crates into the ship's Smelter triggers a timed refining cycle with audio and light escalation, turning the final 30 seconds of every run into a coordinated scramble.
